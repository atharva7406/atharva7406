## Things I'm building

**RideShield**
Shift-based micro-insurance + crash detection for gig workers. Building it as part of our SIH 2026 submission.
`React Native` `FastAPI` `Next.js`

**KhaataKitab**
An agentic AI ledger for small businesses — parses bank SMS into structured transactions, categorizes spending with an on-device Naive Bayes classifier + AI fallback, and tracks cashflow offline-first. Built at Mumbai Hacks.
`React` `TypeScript` `Supabase` `Dexie`

Civic issue reporting platform connecting citizens and local authorities — real-time status tracking, geo-based duplicate detection, and a community leaderboard. Shortlisted for Smart India Hackathon 2025 after clearing the institute-level round.
`Python` `Flask` `PostgreSQL`

`Python` `FastAPI` `Qwen` `Gemma`

**SpendSense**

`Swift` `SwiftUI`
